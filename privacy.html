Another World is a private messenger app. This policy explains what data the app processes and why (GDPR, Art. 13).

2. What data we process

  • Account data: your username and password. The password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and stored there only in hashed form; we never see it. The app uses a technical sign-in identifier derived from your username, not a real e-mail address.
  • Friends data: your friends list and friend requests.
  • Messages: the content of your messages is end-to-end encrypted on your device. We store only the encrypted text and cannot read it. The sender, the recipient and the time of each message are stored unencrypted.
  • Encryption keys: your public key is stored on our server so that friends can send you encrypted messages. Your private key stays on your device and never leaves it.
  • Push notifications (Android): if you allow notifications, a device token is stored so that we can notify you about new messages. The notification does not contain the message text.
  • Technical data: our service providers process technical data such as IP addresses and request logs to run and secure the service.
  • Data on your device: private key, sign-in session, favorites and read status are stored locally in the app.

3. Why and on what legal basis

  • Providing the messenger (account, friends, delivering messages and notifications): Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Security, preventing abuse and technical operation: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Recipients and service providers

  • Supabase (database, authentication, hosting of the backend), project region: [SUPABASE REGION]. We use Supabase as a processor.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivery of push notifications on Android).

Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, this is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, as applicable.

5. How long we keep data

  • Messages are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Account data (username, friends, friend requests, public key) is kept until you delete your account.
  • Your device token is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (home screen, "delete account") or by contacting [YOUR E-MAIL]. This removes your account, your friends list, your device tokens and the messages you sent.
